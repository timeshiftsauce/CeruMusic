Community note fonts

The following fonts are bundled as WOFF2 files converted from their upstream
TrueType releases. Character coverage is retained. Each font is distributed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the corresponding license is in this folder.

Zhi Mang Xing — https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/zhimangxing
Ma Shan Zheng — https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/mashanzheng
Liu Jian Mao Cao — https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/liujianmaocao
Long Cang — https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/longcang
ZCOOL KuaiLe — https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/zcoolkuaile
ZCOOL XiaoWei — https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/zcoolxiaowei
ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou — https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/zcoolqingkehuangyou

The note font pool also reuses the application's existing lyricfont
(Alibaba rounded font) and PingFangSC-Semibold assets and font-face definitions.
